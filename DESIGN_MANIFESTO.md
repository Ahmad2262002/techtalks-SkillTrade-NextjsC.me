# SkillTrade Design Manifesto

> **Status:** LIVING DOCUMENT
> **Role:** CORE DOCTRINE

---

## 0. The North Star
We are building a serious environment for capable humans to exchange skills. This is not a social network. It is not an engagement trap. It is a tool for mastery.

**The Golden Rule:**
If a feature, pixel, or sound does not respect the user's intelligence and time, it is removed.

---

## 1. Emotional Target
The user must feel: **Calm. Respected. In Control.**

- **Think:** A private library, a cockpit, a workshop.
- **Never:** A casino, a circus, a billboard.
- **The Feeling:** "This was designed by people who deeply understand how professionals think."

---

## 2. Visual System
Restraint is our primary aesthetic.

### Density & Whitespace
- **Density is Information, not Clutter.** We do not fear density if the hierarchy is clear.
- **Whitespace is Structure.** It is not just "empty space"; it is the walls that define the room.
- **Grid:** Rigid, mathematical, and predictable. Alignment is godliness.

### Typography
- **Tone:** Objective, Swiss, functional.
- **Role:** Type is for reading, not decoration.
- **Scale:** Use size to denote importance, not volume. Do not shout.

### Color
- **Philosophy:** Color is utility.
- **Palette:** Deep neutrals, tactical accents.
- **Usage:**
    - Backgrounds: Recede.
    - Content: Floats.
    - Actions: Glow (subtly).
    - Errors: Inform, don’t alarm.

---

## 3. Interaction Design
Every click is a commitment.

### Physics
- **Weight:** Interactions should have perceived mass. A button press should feel like a mechanical switch, not a digital trigger.
- **Latency:** Zero. If the system is thinking, the interface acknowledges the input *immediately* before processing.

### Feedback
- **Visual:** Subtle shifts in light and elevation.
- **Auditory:** See Section 5.
- **Success:** A quiet nod. A subtle color shift. No confetti. No pop-ups.
- **Failure:** A clear, unemotional explanation of what went wrong and how to fix it.

---

## 4. Motion & Transitions
Motion is for orientation, not entertainment.

- **Entrance:** Content enters with purpose. No bouncing. 
- **Exit:** Content leaves quickly.
- **Transitions:** You are moving through a space. The spatial relationship between screens must remain consistent.
- **Rule:** If you can't explain why it moves, it should be static.

---

## 5. Sound Scape (Atmosphere Engine)
Silence is the baseline. Sound is the highlight.

- **Role:** To confirm, to alert, to immerse.
- **Quality:** High-fidelity, satisfying, short tones.
- **Inspiration:** Automotive precision, avionics, high-end consumer electronics.
- **Forbidden:** Generic "blips", 8-bit sounds, stock sound effects.

---

## 6. Anti-Patterns (The "Never" List)
Violating these is a failure of design.

1.  **Gamification:** No badges, streaks, or levels that exist purely for engagement.
2.  **Fake Urgency:** No "3 people are looking at this right now".
3.  **Validation Seeking:** No unnecessary "Good job!" messages. The user knows they did a good job.
4.  **Clutter:** No decorative illustrations that serve no functional purpose.

---

## 7. The Long-Term Test
**"Would this embarrass us in five years?"**

If the design relies on a trend (e.g., neumorphism, heavy skeuomorphism, brutalism), it will fail this test. We aim for *timelessness*—design that feels like it has always existed.

---

## 8. Implementation Directives
for Developers & Designers:

- **Commit to the details.** A misaligned pixel is a broken window.
- **Performance is design.** A slow interface is a disrespectful interface.
- **Accessibility is standard.** Serious tools work for everyone.
- **Question everything.** If a requirement complicates the interface, push back on the requirement.

---

*Design with humility. Design with taste. Design with responsibility.*
